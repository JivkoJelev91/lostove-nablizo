# Street Fitness

Find somewhere to train, right now.

Street Fitness is a mobile app for finding outdoor calisthenics spots — the pull-up bars, dip
stations, gym rings, monkey bars and ladders you find in a park or on the street, rather than
equipment you had to walk to a gym for.

Browse spots near you and see what each one actually has: what equipment is there, in what
condition, how many of it, photos, and what other people thought of it. Save the ones worth
coming back to, and add the ones that are missing — the app fills in the position from where you
are standing, so adding a spot is about as much work as tapping one button.

## Features

- Browse spots and the equipment each one has
- Add a new spot, with your current location and photos
- Rate and review spots you have trained at
- Save spots to your favourites
- Report a spot that is out of date or wrong
- Light and dark appearance, following the system

## Status

Work in progress. The app is being built on Expo and React Native, with Supabase as the backend.
Sign-in is not wired up yet, and the database schema exists but its access policies are still
being written, so the app has no data access at the moment.
